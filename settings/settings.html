Detection & Scanner Behavior

Automatically inspects pages for password inputs and triggers security verification without reading passwords.

Display a modern floating security banner in the top-right corner when visiting login pages.

Query public RDAP servers to check registration creation dates and flag newly registered domains.

Cross-reference target domains against Google Safe Browsing and curated phishing databases.

Trusted Domains (Whitelist)

Suppress heuristic warnings for verified domains you regularly use (e.g., your college portal or workplace login).

Important Security Rule: Adding a domain to your whitelist suppresses heuristic warnings only. It will never suppress a confirmed threat if the site is compromised or listed on phishing blocklists.

External Threat Intelligence APIs

Stored locally in Chrome storage. Never transmitted to third parties or embedded in extension source code.

Points to your private LoginGuard Express.js backend server (e.g. http://localhost:3000 or your deployed URL).

Data & Privacy Controls

LoginGuard Privacy Commitment

LoginGuard was built from the ground up to respect user privacy:

  • Zero Credential Access: Content scripts inspect DOM tag types only. They never access input.value, listen to keystrokes, or intercept form submissions.
  • Local-First Heuristics: HTTPS encryption, Punycode detection, and lookalike algorithms run 100% locally on your machine.
  • Minimum Metadata: Only the domain name and normalized URL are checked against RDAP or threat feeds.
  • All Data Local: Scan history is saved exclusively in your browser's local storage and can be deleted at any time.

Permanently erase all locally stored domain audit logs.
